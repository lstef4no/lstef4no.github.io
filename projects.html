Nonlinear Model Predictive Control (NMPC) for high-performance virtual car drivers

The project develops a Nonlinear Model Predictive Control for high-performance virtual car drivers, addressing complex vehicle dynamics at the limits of stability. The controller leverages a full nonlinear vehicle model with tire dynamics and lateral-longitudinal coupling, solved through real-time optimization under actuator and safety constraints, achieving near-optimal racing trajectories and robust stability.

Bosch Future Mobility Challenge

Participated in an international student competition on autonomous driving and connectivity for 1/10 scale vehicles. Designed algorithms for autonomous navigation in smart cities, obstacle avoidance, and V2X communication, and collaborated in a multidisciplinary team to integrate perception, planning, and control modules.

Master’s Thesis Internship

Developed an enhanced Kinodynamic RRT algorithm for online path planning of small fixed-wing UAVs in obstacle-rich environments. The approach incorporated both kinematic and dynamic constraints, producing collision-free and dynamically feasible trajectories. Validated the method with real-world UAV flight tests, demonstrating robustness and applicability to surveillance, monitoring, and disaster response.
