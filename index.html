A little about me

Hi, I’m
Stefano.

I develop embedded software and firmware, with an academic background in control systems.

My interests centre on control, estimation, and signal processing, with embedded systems connecting those ideas to real hardware.

I’m particularly drawn to mobile robotics, aerospace and space systems, and audio. I’m also curious about human–machine systems, including brain–computer interfaces and exoskeletons—areas I’d like to explore further.

What keeps

↗Robotics & aerospace

02 / Background

The foundations

Master’s degree

Control Systems Engineering

A focus on dynamical systems, optimization, estimation, and advanced control, including nonlinear and model predictive control. Coursework connected the theory to robotics, autonomous systems, and data-driven methods, with laboratory work bringing those ideas into practice.

Bachelor’s degree

Electronic and Computer Engineering

A broad foundation in electronics, computer science, and automation, covering circuits, programming, signal processing, and control. These studies strengthened my mathematical foundations and analytical approach to understanding electronic, software, and dynamic systems.
